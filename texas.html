Texas Roadhouse Review

☆4/5

I ordered the sirloin and grilled shrimp combo that comes with two sides - I chose the caesar salad and loaded baked potato that comes with bacon, cheese, and sour cream. The caesar salad was very good - crisp romain lettuce, crunchy croutons, and the creamy savory caesar dressing and parmesan. The dressing was tangy - added a nice touch to the flavor.

The sirloin steak was alright, for being a steak house. I ordered a medium well cooked steak, and it came back to me cooked all the way through with almost no pink, and took a long time to chew and eat. My advice is to order it less cooked than you are wanting it, for it to come out perfect how you'd like it.

The shrimp was the best part in my opinion. It was seasoned very well, a good blend of herbs and spices, hints of garlic lemon pepper and very zesty and tender.

Texas Roadhouse is one of the most filling restaurants I have every been to. My other piece of advice is to eat LESS of their fresh bakes rolls, or not order appetizer. Overall 4/5
