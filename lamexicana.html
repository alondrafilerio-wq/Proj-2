La Mexicana Review

☆4/5

La Mexicana opened back in 2021, and is located at 1701 2nd Street Suite 4 Coralville, IA 52241. They are a Mexican restaurant, with a big range of foods to try. From tacos to pescado, breakfast to dinner, they have it all. Weekdays open from 11-8pm, and weekends vary on day.

I ordered Tacos de carne asada, as you could never go wrong with a classic mexican dish, everything included - onion, cilantro and salsas on the side. Without salsa, the tortilla and meat were dry, not much to see there. I then added on the light green salsa, which had a taste of jalapeno, and it added a nice touch to the tacos, along with some lime.

Another good thing about La Mexicana is their flan. Its texture was smooth, silky and creamy. It tasted very sweet, caramelized, vanilla and milky. Flan from the Mexicana is one of my favoritest things to get there. Everytime someone in my family goes, I always request for them to bring it back for me.
