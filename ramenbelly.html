Ramen Belly Review

☆4/5

Ramen Belly is located at 1010 Martin St, Iowa City, IA 52245 and opened back in 2021. They are a Japanese restaurant, with main dishes being Ramen, and rice bowls. A favorite appetizer of ours is their pot stickers. One order comes with 4 pot stickers, a side of cucumbers and topped off in a sauce. We got two orders because we already know what we like, and sometimes only come for the pot stickers. 10 out of 10 highly recommend.

Juliana and I went to try it out. Juliana ordered the ramen in a blanket, which is ramen noodles in two pieces of tonkatsu - pork. It comes in a really creamy sauce that tastes like chicken maruchan seasoning, but it tastes original, like something you couldn't just microwave at home. Overall 8.5/ 10.

I got the Tonkatsu belly bowl. It is a sticky rice bowl, served with tonkatsu slices on the top with cucumbers on the side, topped off with adobo sauce. The cucumber had a nice freshness to it, and the rice was sticky, but not completely, you could still take it apart. The tonkatsu was crispy and had a lot of sauce on it, but overall was a 9/10 because of all the sauce. I would recommend coming here, it is a little shop with not too many visitors.
